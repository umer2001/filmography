# Character Continuity

## 1. Stable Visual Anchors
- Must-stay wardrobe markers: Navy diner uniform in post-shift scenes
- Stable physical traits: Dark tied-back hair, tired eyes, practical presentation
- Stable carrying items: Folded work clothes or small practical belongings when coming off shift

## 2. Stable Behavioral Anchors
- Speech rules: Speaks briefly and avoids over-explaining
- Emotional expression rules: Feelings surface through timing and restraint more than volume
- Things the character would not suddenly do: Become playful, theatrical, or openly melodramatic without story justification

## 3. Story-State Anchors
- Relationship state at current phase: Hurt but still responsive to proof
- Knowledge the character has: Knows Ray disappeared with the cassette and has not fully accounted for it yet
- Story facts tied to the character: She does not fully forgive Ray in the laundromat scene

## 4. Drift Risks
- What later scenes or prompts might accidentally change: Turning Sana into a more verbally expressive or instantly forgiving version of herself
