# Character Profile

## 1. Character Identity
- Name: Sana
- Project: Borrowed Light
- Role in story: Primary emotional point-of-view character

## 2. Narrative Role
- Core purpose in the story: Show how trust is withheld, tested, and partly reopened
- Main want: To avoid being hurt again while preserving self-respect
- Main fear: That repair will ask her to pretend the damage was small
- Arc across the screenplay: Moves from shutdown and routine toward cautious re-entry into shared possibility

## 3. Visual Identity
- Appearance: Dark hair usually tied back loosely, tired eyes, practical posture
- Wardrobe markers: Navy diner uniform after shifts, plain outerwear, minimal styling
- Physical habits: Folds clothing carefully, pauses before answering, watches before speaking

## 4. Voice And Behavior
- Dialogue style: Sparse, direct, emotionally economical
- Behavioral traits: Observant, controlled, withholding until action proves intent
- Emotional default: Guarded

## 5. Story-Relevant Relationships
- Key relationships: Ray
- Important tensions or loyalties: Shared history and unfinished collaboration create emotional stakes

## 6. Notes For Downstream Work
- What scene-level workflows need to remember: Sana should not soften too early; silence is often part of her dramatic language
