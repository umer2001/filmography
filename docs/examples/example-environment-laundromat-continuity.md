# Environment Continuity

## 1. Stable Visual Anchors
- Lighting rules: Keep the laundromat cool and fluorescent, not warmly romanticized
- Palette rules: Muted blues, grays, and practical whites
- Spatial markers: Folding counter, washer row, front windows, entry bell

## 2. Stable Atmosphere Anchors
- Sound or ambience expectations: Washer hum, fluorescent buzz, rain against glass
- Weather or time continuity: Night and rain remain part of the emotional frame
- Emotional tone: Quiet, suspended, intimate without becoming dreamlike

## 3. Story-State Anchors
- What is always true here: It is a working everyday space, not a stylized symbolic set
- What changes over time: Occupancy may vary, but emotional charge comes from who enters the room

## 4. Drift Risks
- What later scenes or prompts might accidentally change: Overfilling the space, warming the palette too much, or making it feel glossy rather than lived-in
