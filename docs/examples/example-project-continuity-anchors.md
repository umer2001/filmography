# Project Continuity Anchors

## 1. Character Anchors
- Sana stays emotionally restrained, visually tired, and practically dressed
- Ray stays sincere, hesitant, and slightly weather-beaten rather than polished

## 2. Environment Anchors
- The visual world stays grounded, contemporary, and urban
- Night spaces lean cool, fluorescent, and rain-touched

## 3. Relationship Anchors
- Sana and Ray remain emotionally unresolved through most of the story
- Small acts matter more than speeches

## 4. Story Element Anchors
- The cassette is a recurring symbol of trust, damage, and repair
- Rain recurs as a motif of suspension and delayed motion

## 5. Tone And Visual Anchors
- Grounded realism over stylized fantasy
- Intimacy through stillness, observation, and low-key spaces

## 6. Things That Must Not Drift
- Sana does not forgive too quickly
- Ray's return is sincere, not manipulative
- The cassette never becomes visually pristine
