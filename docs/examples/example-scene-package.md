## 2. Scene Purpose
- Story function: Reconnect two estranged characters through a small act of return.
- Why this scene exists: It turns distance into tentative trust.
- What changes in this scene: Sana stops holding Ray outside the moment.
- Outcome by end of scene: Sana allows Ray to stay.

## 3. Story Context
- What happens immediately before: Sana closes the diner and returns alone to the all-night laundromat she uses to avoid going home.
- What happens immediately after: The pair leave together with the recovered cassette and the unresolved future now shared instead of avoided.
- What the audience already knows: Ray disappeared weeks earlier after losing the demo tape Sana trusted him to deliver.
- What new information this scene delivers: Ray found the damaged tape and came back rather than running again.

## 4. Scene Summary
Late at night in a nearly empty laundromat, Sana folds work clothes beneath harsh fluorescent lights while rain hits the front windows. The bell over the door rings and Ray enters, soaked and carrying the dented cassette case he once lost. He says he found it. Sana does not answer at first, forcing him to sit with the apology. When he places the tape on the folding counter and admits he should have come back sooner, Sana takes the cassette, looks at him, and quietly says, "Then stay."

## 5. Emotional Arc
- Starting emotional state: Guarded, exhausted, self-protective
- Key emotional turns: Shock at Ray's return; refusal to reward him immediately; softening once the cassette is physically returned
- Ending emotional state: Tentative openness
- Intended audience feeling at the end: Relief edged with uncertainty
