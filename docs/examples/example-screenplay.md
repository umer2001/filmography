## 3. Full Screenplay

### SC-01 - INT. DINER - NIGHT

Sana wipes down the counter after close while a cassette player behind the register clicks uselessly with no tape inside. Her coworker asks if she's coming out after shift. Sana says no without looking up.

### SC-02 - EXT. BUS STOP - NIGHT

Rain starts. Sana stands alone under the shelter and watches a bus pass without getting on it.

### SC-03 - INT. APARTMENT HALLWAY - NIGHT

Ray arrives at his apartment building and stops before going in. He opens his messenger bag, sees the dented cassette case, and closes the bag again.

### SC-04 - INT. LAUNDROMAT - NIGHT

Sana folds uniforms beneath fluorescent lights while rain streaks the front windows. The bell over the door rings. Ray enters, soaked, carrying the dented cassette case.

RAY
I found it.

Sana does not answer.

Ray crosses to the folding counter and sets the cassette down.

RAY
I should've come back sooner.

Sana picks up the cassette, studies the dent, then looks at him.

SANA
Then stay.
