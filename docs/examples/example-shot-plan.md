# Shot Plan

## 1. Scene Identity
- Project: Borrowed Light
- Scene ID: SC-04
- Scene Title: Laundromat Promise
- Version: v1

## 2. Coverage Strategy
- Scene objective on screen: Let the audience feel Sana holding emotional control until the physical return of the cassette changes the room.
- Dominant emotional rhythm: Quiet tension interrupted by one concrete act of repair.
- Coverage philosophy: Stay close enough to feel intimacy, but let the room and machine rhythm keep the scene grounded.
- Scene-level prompt style family: `cinematic-atmospheric`
- Secondary prompt style families: `character-dialogue`, `product-showcase`
- Must-show beats: Ray's entrance, cassette reveal, cassette handoff, Sana's final line
- Can-imply beats: How Ray searched, what either character is imagining internally

## 3. Continuity Anchors
- Character anchors: Sana's navy uniform and folded sleeves; Ray's wet red hoodie and messenger bag
- Environment anchors: Rain-striped front windows, rotating washer lights, metal folding counter
- Prop anchors: Dented cassette case stays visible once revealed
- Camera-language anchors: Controlled movement, restrained push-ins, no abrupt stylistic changes
- Lighting/style anchors: Fluorescent overhead coolness with warm skin tones and neon reflections

## 5. Reference-Planning Notes
- References required before prompt work: Sana character look, Ray wet look, laundromat style frame, cassette prop close-up
- Reusable references across the scene: Character references and environment style frame
- Start/end frame needs: S2 needs bridge frames for the stitched seam between Ray entering and crossing to the counter

## 6. Sound-Design Notes
- Dialogue-critical moments: S2 line "I found it."; S4 apology; S5 "Then stay."
- Ambience-critical moments: S1 and S3 need washers and rain to support silence
- Cue-driven moments: Door bell at S2 entry; cassette contact on counter in S4

## 7. Prompt-Generation Notes
- Likely single-prompt shots: S1, S3, S4
- Likely stitched multi-prompt shots: S2
- Likely timestamped multi-beat sequences: S5
- Prompt style selection notes: Use `cinematic-atmospheric` for isolation and room mood, `character-dialogue` for Ray/Sana performance and locked lines, and `product-showcase` for the cassette placement because the prop is the proof of repair.
- Main prompt continuity risks: Maintaining wardrobe wetness, cassette position, and consistent rain/reflection look across adjacent shots
