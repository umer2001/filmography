# Story Scene

## 1. Scene Identity
- Project: Borrowed Light
- Scene ID: SC-04
- Scene Title: Laundromat Promise

## 2. Scene Metadata
- Scene purpose: Reconnect Sana and Ray through a physical act of return rather than explanation
- Characters present: Sana, Ray
- Story continuity in: Sana is still guarded; Ray has already chosen to come back with the cassette
- Story continuity out: They are not fully reconciled, but the future is shared again
- Environment: Laundromat
- Time of day: Night

## 3. Full Screenplay Scene Text

Sana folds uniforms beneath fluorescent lights while rain streaks the front windows. The bell over the door rings. Ray enters, soaked, carrying the dented cassette case.

RAY
I found it.

Sana does not answer.

Ray crosses to the folding counter and sets the cassette down.

RAY
I should've come back sooner.

Sana picks up the cassette, studies the dent, then looks at him.

SANA
Then stay.
