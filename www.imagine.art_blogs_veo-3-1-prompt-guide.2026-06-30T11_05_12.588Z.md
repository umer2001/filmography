## What is Veo 3.1?

[Veo 3.1](https://www.imagine.art/features/veo-3-1-ai-video-generator) builds on Veo 3 with stronger prompt adherence and improved audiovisual quality. According to Google's official documentation, the model excels at generating realistic, synchronized sound, from multi-person conversations to precisely timed sound effects.

Key capabilities include:

- **Realistic physics simulation** that understands how objects move and interact in real-world scenarios
- **Reference image uploads** to maintain consistent characters, objects, or visual styles across multiple clips
- **First and last frame features** to define both the beginning and ending of your video, with Veo 3.1 creating natural transitions between them
- **Professional-grade audio generation** with dialogue, sound effects, and ambient sounds

## The Effective Veo 3.1 Prompt Formula

Every effective Veo 3.1 prompt follows a clear five-part formula:

[Shot Composition] + [Subject Details] + [Action] + [Setting/Environment] +[Aesthetics/Mood]

1. **Shot composition:** Describe camera angle, position, and movement
2. **Subject details:** Add specific details about your subject
3. **Action:** Describe what's taking place
4. **Setting:** Define environmental details and atmosphere
5. **Aesthetics:** Specify visual style, lighting, depth of field, and overall mood

The most effective prompts run about 3 to 6 sentences or 100 to 150 words.

## Final Thoughts

The 25 prompts in this guide come directly from verified creator testing. Use them as templates and modify the specific details while keeping the overall structure intact. Pay attention to how each prompt balances shot composition, subject description, action, setting, aesthetics, and audio elements.

Key takeaways:

- Always use the five-part formula for consistent results
- Include specific audio instructions to leverage Veo 3.1's synchronized sound
- Avoid vague language, be specific and descriptive
- Specify camera movement and shot type in every prompt
- Use 100-150 words for optimal prompt length
- Add "no subtitles" if you don't want text overlays
